Manufacturer & Installer

Koyee Container Workshop has built a strong reputation through its successful delivery of high-quality camp accommodation and temporary facility solutions including various QATAR ENERGY and ARAMCO projects. Moving beyond conventional portacabin and flat- pack systems, Koyee has developed its own proprietary modular container building system.
Supported by our parent factory in China and local facilities in Qatar and the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, KOYEE is well positioned to meet the most demanding project requirements.
We have a proven record of delivering and installing 10,000-bed camps in just 125 days in ARAMCO and QATAR ENERGY Projects.

With a reputation built on quality, speed, and reliability, KOYEE is your trusted partner for modular infrastructure across the Middle East Region.

OUR APPROACH

Empowering Sustainable Growth in Modular Construction

At Koyee, we are committed to driving sustainable growth in the construction and infrastructure sectors through advanced modular solutions. Our innovative container systems reduce on-site waste, optimize build times, and offer long-term flexibility—helping clients scale faster while minimizing environmental impact.

To lead the transformation of modular construction by delivering world-class container-based infrastructure that is fast, flexible, and built to last. We envision a future where high-quality, prefabricated solutions become the standard for temporary and permanent buildings across industries.

At Koyee, our mission is to design and deliver high-performance modular container solutions that serve the evolving needs of industrial, commercial, and humanitarian sectors. Backed by advanced manufacturing, a skilled team, and regional presence in Saudi Arabia and Qatar, we aim to:

  • Provide durable, scalable, and eco-friendly building systems.
  • Deliver on-time, cost-effective solutions across diverse environments.
  • Maintain the highest standards of engineering and service.
  • Empower clients with end-to-end support—from concept to completion.

  • Integrity – We believe in honesty, transparency, and ethical practices in everything we do.

  • Excellence – We pursue the highest standards in design, quality, and delivery..

  • Innovation – We continuously improve our modular systems to stay ahead of industry needs.

  • Reliability – We deliver on time, every time—no matter the scale or location.

  • Customer Commitment – We build long-term relationships by understanding and delivering what our clients truly need.
